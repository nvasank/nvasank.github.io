KEERTHI VASAN — GITHUB PAGES

1. Extract this ZIP on your computer.
2. Open https://github.com/nvasank/nvasank.github.io
3. Click Add file > Upload files.
4. Upload index.html, keerthi-vasan.jpg and Keerthi_Vasan_Resume.pdf directly into the repository root.
   Do not upload the ZIP itself or a containing folder.
5. Click Commit changes and commit directly to main.
6. Wait for the Pages deployment to complete under Actions.
7. Open https://nvasank.github.io/ and refresh.

Settings > Pages should use Deploy from a branch, main, / (root).
You can preview locally by opening index.html alongside the PDF.
